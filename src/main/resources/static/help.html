Help & FAQs

Answers to the questions shoppers and shop owners ask most. Can’t find yours? Contact us.

Ordering

Which areas do you deliver to?

Each shop chooses the PIN codes it serves. Enter your PIN code at the top of any page to check. Right now NammaBasket covers Bengaluru PIN codes starting with 560.

Is there a minimum order or a delivery fee?

The delivery fee and any free-delivery amount are shown in your cart before you pay. Nothing is added after checkout.

Can I change or cancel an order?

You can cancel from My orders until the shop starts packing. A paid order that you cancel is refunded in full.

An item was missing from my delivery. What do I do?

Open the order in My orders and use Something missing or wrong? within 48 hours of delivery. You’re refunded for the missing items, including your share of any coupon.

Payments

How can I pay?

Cash on delivery, or UPI. NammaBasket is a portfolio project, so the UPI screen is a demo: no money is ever taken, and you should never enter real bank details.

Why did my total change before I paid?

If a shop changes a price after you add an item, your cart shows the new price and asks you to confirm it. You’re never charged a price you didn’t see.

Your account

I forgot my password.

Password reset by email isn’t available yet. Contact us from the email address on your account and we’ll help.

Why is my account locked?

After 5 wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes to stop someone guessing it. Wait, then try again.

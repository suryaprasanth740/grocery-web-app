How NammaBasket works

  1. Find shops near you

    Enter your PIN code and see the kirana stores that deliver to your area, with their hours and delivery fee.

  2. Fill one basket

    Add groceries from one shop or several. Your basket keeps each shop’s items, fees and total separate.

  3. The shop packs it

    Each shop accepts your order and packs it. Track it until it reaches your door, or collect it yourself.

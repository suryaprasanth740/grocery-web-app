Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

NammaBasket is a portfolio project. This page explains, in plain words, what it stores about you and why.

What we store

  • Your account: name, email address, and your password as a one-way BCrypt hash. Nobody, including us, can read your password.
  • Your orders: the items, prices, delivery address and PIN code you enter at checkout, so the shop can deliver and you can see your order history.
  • Your cart and budget: items in your basket and the monthly budget, if you set one.
  • Problem reports: what you tell us when you report a missing item.

What we don’t store

  • Card numbers, UPI PINs or bank details. Payments are a demo and never ask for them.
  • Your exact location. If you let the site use your location, it is only used in your browser to find your PIN code area.

Who sees your details

A shop sees your name, delivery address and the items you ordered from that shop, only for its own orders. NammaBasket admins can see orders to handle problems and refunds. Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Cookies and browser storage

One cookie keeps you logged in. It can’t be read by page scripts and is only sent to NammaBasket. Your PIN code is remembered in your browser’s local storage so you don’t have to type it on every visit. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

To have your account and orders deleted, contact us.
